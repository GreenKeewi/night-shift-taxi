HOW TO PLAY

01 / FEEL THE CAB

W or ↑ accelerates. A/D or ←/→ steers. S or ↓ brakes, then reverses. Space is a strong brake. Steering reverses naturally when backing up.

02 / EARN THE FARE

Follow the mint route to the glowing bay. Park slowly, line up with the arrow and hold still. Riders get in and out automatically.

03 / PARK LIKE A PRO

Parallel bays have cars on both ends. Approach from the road and ease into the gap. Reverse bays point toward the street: nose out, back in at least a car's half-length.

04 / READ THE STREETS

Drive on the right. Signals cycle red, amber and green. Entering on red costs $12 and 5 seconds. The bottom expressway has two lanes each way and merging slip roads.

05 / KEEP YOUR SHIFT

Finish fares for cash, tips and small repairs. Traffic follows morning, evening and late-night rush waves. Deadlines tighten with each fare. Three missed fares or a wrecked cab ends your shift. There are no levels.

06 / KEYBOARD ALL THE WAY

Esc/P pauses. R calls a tow for $15 and 10 seconds. L toggles 30 FPS low power. M toggles sound. Menus use ↑/↓ and Enter. New city generates a new map.
